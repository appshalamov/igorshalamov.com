About Igor Shalamov

Igor Shalamov (Russian: Игорь Шаламов) is an entrepreneur and AI software architect working at the intersection of machine learning, product analytics and marketing.

Biography

Igor Shalamov is an entrepreneur and AI software architect working at the intersection of machine learning, product analytics and marketing. In 2021 he launched Shalamov.io, a referral-marketing automation platform that lets businesses build referral funnels, Telegram services and Mini Apps, as well as no-code AI modules for segmentation, analytics and customer conversations. The platform anchors an academy and an implementation agency.

From 2021 to 2023 Igor led referral-systems development at the Like Center education ecosystem; since 2022 he has been responsible for referral marketing and IT development at the Web3 accelerator LaunchHub; and since 2023 he has worked with Appvillis, the company behind the Nicegram messenger, on AI-driven feedback automation and content filtering. He is the author of research articles on semantic news search, real-time neural news classification and neural architectures for referral funnels. He is now building the international Shalamov.io ecosystem and the Shalamov AI product.

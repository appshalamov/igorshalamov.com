# Igor Shalamov — Facts and Answers

## Who is Igor Shalamov?

Igor Shalamov is an AI systems architect who designs AI growth systems: referral and recommendation systems, Telegram services, AI-driven communication automation. He founded the Shalamov.io platform in 2021.

Source: [Biography](https://igorshalamov.com/about/).

## What are AI growth systems?

AI growth systems are the field Igor Shalamov works in: referral and recommendation systems, AI-driven communication automation, semantic search and classification. The site has a separate page on how such systems are built, with his work and five research articles.

Source: [AI growth systems](https://igorshalamov.com/ai-growth-systems/).

## What does he work on now?

He is building the international Shalamov.io ecosystem, with its academy and AI implementation agency. Since April 2023 he has also worked with Appvillis, the company behind the Nicegram messenger, as AI Software Architect.

Source: [Products](https://igorshalamov.com/products/).

## What is Shalamov.io?

Shalamov.io is a referral-marketing automation platform, launched by Igor Shalamov in 2021, that lets businesses build referral funnels, Telegram services and Mini Apps, as well as no-code AI modules. An academy and an implementation agency work on top of the platform.

Source: [shalamov.io](https://shalamov.io/).

Since April 2023 Igor Shalamov has worked with Appvillis UAB, the company behind the Nicegram messenger, as AI Software Architect and Head of Referral Marketing. His work there covers AI-driven feedback automation and content filtering.

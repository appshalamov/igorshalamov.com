Igor Shalamov — entrepreneur and AI software architect

Igor Shalamov is an entrepreneur and AI software architect focused on growth systems. He founded Shalamov.io, a platform for referral funnels and Telegram services, plus an academy and an AI implementation agency. He has designed referral and recommendation systems for education and Web3 platforms and worked on AI automation within the Nicegram messaging ecosystem. He writes on semantic search and neural news classification.

Areas of work

AI systems architecture

Since April 2023 Igor Shalamov has worked with Appvillis, the company behind the Nicegram messenger, as AI Software Architect and Head of Referral Marketing. The work covers AI-driven feedback automation and content filtering.

Growth systems

Referral and recommendation systems for education and Web3 platforms: lead architect of referral systems at Like Center (2021–2023), head of referral marketing and IT development at the Web3 accelerator LaunchHub (since 2022).

Applied AI for content

Research articles on semantic news search, real-time neural news classification, news personalization and neural network architectures for referral and sales funnels. See Research.

Products

Shalamov.io (since 2021) is a referral-marketing automation platform with an academy and an AI implementation agency. Shalamov AI, an AI aggregator, is in development. Woso is an AI platform for images, video, voice and music. See Products.

Igor Shalamov — AI systems architect

AI systems architect and founder of Shalamov.io. He designs AI growth systems: referral and recommendation systems, AI-driven communication automation, semantic search and classification.

Igor Shalamov is an AI systems architect and entrepreneur who designs AI growth systems. He founded Shalamov.io, a platform for referral funnels and Telegram services, plus an academy and an AI implementation agency. He has designed referral and recommendation systems for education and Web3 platforms and worked on AI automation within the Nicegram messaging ecosystem. He writes on semantic search and neural news classification.

Areas of work

AI growth systems

Referral and recommendation systems, AI-driven communication automation, semantic search and classification: what this class of systems is, how it is built, and Igor Shalamov's work and research in it.

AI systems architecture at Appvillis (Nicegram)

Since April 2023 Igor Shalamov has worked with Appvillis, the company behind the Nicegram messenger, as AI Software Architect and Head of Referral Marketing. The work covers AI-driven feedback automation and content filtering.

Referral and recommendation systems

Referral and recommendation systems for education and Web3 platforms: lead architect of referral systems at Like Center (2021–2023), head of referral marketing and IT development at the Web3 accelerator LaunchHub (since 2022).

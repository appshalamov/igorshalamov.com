Medium bio, about 150 words

Igor Shalamov is an AI systems architect and entrepreneur working at the intersection of machine learning, product analytics and marketing. In 2021 he launched Shalamov.io, a referral-marketing automation platform that lets businesses build referral funnels, Telegram services and Mini Apps, as well as no-code AI modules for segmentation, analytics and customer conversations. The platform anchors an academy and an implementation agency.

From 2021 to 2023 Igor led referral-systems development at the Like Center education ecosystem; since 2022 he has been responsible for referral marketing and IT development at the Web3 accelerator LaunchHub; and since April 2023 he has worked with Appvillis, the company behind the Nicegram messenger, as AI Software Architect on AI-driven feedback automation and content filtering. He is the author of research articles on semantic news search, real-time neural news classification, news personalization and neural architectures for referral funnels. He is now building the international Shalamov.io ecosystem.

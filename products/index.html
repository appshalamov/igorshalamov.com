Products — Shalamov.io, Shalamov AI and Woso

Shalamov.io

Shalamov.io is a referral-marketing automation platform, launched by Igor Shalamov in 2021, that lets businesses build referral funnels, Telegram services and Mini Apps, as well as no-code AI modules for segmentation, analytics and customer conversations. Igor Shalamov is the founder and platform architect.

shalamov.io

Shalamov.io Academy

The academy of the platform trains specialists in AI automation. Founded in 2021; Igor Shalamov is the founder and an instructor.

Shalamov Agency

An AI implementation agency founded by Igor Shalamov in 2020.

Shalamov AI

An AI aggregator. Igor Shalamov has been the product architect since 2025. The product is in development.

Woso

Woso is an AI platform that brings image, video, voice, music and chat models together in one place. Its agent prepares ads, videos, voice-overs and music from a text description.

Research and Publications

Igor Shalamov, an AI systems architect and founder of Shalamov.io, is the author of research articles on semantic news search, real-time neural news classification, AI-based news personalization and neural network architectures for referral and sales funnels. Together they cover the main parts of AI growth systems. Each article has its own page with citation data.

  1. Семантический поиск новостей с использованием искусственного интеллекта: технологии, вызовы и перспективы

    English title: Semantic news search using artificial intelligence: technologies, problems and prospects. Article in Russian.

    Aktualnye issledovaniya (Current Research), APNI, No. 20 (202). Published 16 May 2024. ISSN 2713-1513.

    Article on the journal site. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17215118.

  2. Нейросетевые архитектуры для автоматизации пользовательских воронок в реферальном маркетинге

    English title: Neural network architectures for automating user funnels in referral marketing. Article in Russian.

    Aktualnye issledovaniya (Current Research), APNI, No. 29 (264), part I, pp. 29–38. Published 24 July 2025. ISSN 2713-1513.

    Article on the journal site.

  3. AI-driven маркетинг: эволюция воронок продаж в цифровых экосистемах

    English title: AI-driven marketing: the evolution of sales funnels in digital ecosystems. Article in Russian.

    Internauka (electronic scientific journal), No. 25 (389), section “Information technologies”. Published 2025.

    Article on the journal site. Author page in the journal.

  4. Нейронные сети для классификации новостей в реальном времени: вычислительные подходы, приложения и вызовы

    English title: Neural networks for real-time news classification: computational approaches, applications and open problems. Article in Russian.

    Internauka (electronic scientific journal), No. 35 (399), section “Information technologies”. Published 2025.

    Article on the journal site. Author page in the journal.

  5. Personalization of news content using AI: architecture and efficiency of neural network systems

    European Journal of Technical and Natural Sciences, Premier Publishing s.r.o., No. 4–5 (2025), pp. 18–29. Published 30 October 2025. ISSN 2414-2352.

    Article on the journal site. DOI: 10.29013/EJTNS-25-4.5-18-29.

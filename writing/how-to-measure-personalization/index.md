# How to measure personalization

[Igor Shalamov](https://igorshalamov.com/about/), an AI systems architect and founder of Shalamov.io. 21 November 2026, 1 min read.

My paper on [personalization of news content with AI](https://igorshalamov.com/research/news-personalization-ai/) uses ranking metrics, and they deserve a plain explanation.

- **Precision@5**: of the first five items shown, how many were relevant.
- **CTR**: the share of shown items that were opened.
- **Coverage**: how much of the catalogue the system ever recommends.
- **Freshness**: how old the recommended items are.

A system tuned only for CTR drifts toward clickbait. A system tuned only for precision can become narrow and repetitive. Good personalization reports several metrics together and makes the trade-offs visible.

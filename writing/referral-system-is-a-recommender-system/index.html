A referral system is a recommender system

Igor Shalamov, an AI systems architect and founder of Shalamov.io. 20 November 2026, 1 min read.

The first thing I tell teams about referral programs is this: a referral program is a recommender system in disguise.

It has to answer three questions for every user:

  1. Who is likely to invite someone right now?
  2. Whom will they invite, and in which channel?
  3. Which message and which reward will make that invitation land?

Each question is a ranking problem. Treat referrals as a static “invite a friend” button and you leave all three to chance. Treat them as recommendations and you can learn from every invitation that was sent, opened or ignored.

A referral funnel as an AI growth system

Read this way, a referral funnel has the three layers of an AI growth system: invitations are the communication, their outcomes are the behaviour data, and the ranking models close the loop.

What the research covers

My paper Neural network architectures for automating user funnels in referral marketing (Aktualnye issledovaniya, 2025) reviews the architectures used for this work: large language models, convolutional, recurrent and generative adversarial networks, and reinforcement learning models. They are applied to content generation, audience analysis, interaction with users and campaign optimisation. The paper also discusses the ethical, privacy and technical sides of putting neural networks into referral funnels.

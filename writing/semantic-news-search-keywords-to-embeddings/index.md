# Semantic news search: from keywords to embeddings

[Igor Shalamov](https://igorshalamov.com/about/), an AI systems architect and founder of Shalamov.io. 20 November 2026, 1 min read.

Keyword search finds documents that share words with the query. Semantic search finds documents that share meaning. The switch is simple to describe: encode queries and documents as vectors (embeddings) and retrieve by distance instead of by term overlap.

## What changes for news

- Synonyms and paraphrases stop being a problem.
- A story can be found before it has a fixed name.
- The index must be refreshed as fast as the news itself, which makes freshness an architecture question, not a model question.

## What the research covers

My paper [Semantic news search using artificial intelligence: technologies, problems and prospects](https://igorshalamov.com/research/semantic-news-search-ai/) (Aktualnye issledovaniya, 2024) reviews the technologies behind this switch: vector databases, hybrid and cascade approaches, contextual retrieval. It looks at the open problems, namely scalability, real-time processing, integration with media infrastructure and security, and at the directions that follow: agent-oriented interfaces, multimodal search, personalization and federated systems.

In an [AI growth system](https://igorshalamov.com/writing/what-is-an-ai-growth-system/), semantic search sits in the models layer: it decides what a reader sees next.

# Telegram Mini Apps as a recommendation surface

[Igor Shalamov](https://igorshalamov.com/about/), an AI systems architect and founder of Shalamov.io. 21 November 2026, 1 min read.

Shalamov.io, which I founded, is a platform for referral funnels, Telegram services and Mini Apps with no-code AI modules.

A Mini App inside a messenger is an unusual place for recommendations: the user arrives from a chat, a channel or a friend's link, often with clear intent and very little patience.

## Design consequences

- The entry point is a feature: the link a person came from tells you a lot.
- The first screen must already be personalised.
- The invitation flow lives one tap away, inside the same conversation.

That makes the Mini App both a storefront and a [referral surface](https://igorshalamov.com/writing/referral-system-is-a-recommender-system/).

# What is an AI growth system

[Igor Shalamov](https://igorshalamov.com/about/), an AI systems architect and founder of Shalamov.io. 20 November 2026, 1 min read.

An AI growth system is the class of systems that help a digital product grow by learning from its users: referral and recommendation systems, AI-driven communication automation, semantic search and classification.

## Three layers

Every such system has three layers:

1. **Behaviour data**: what people open, share, ignore and reply to.
2. **Models**: recommendation and ranking, classification of intent and sentiment.
3. **Communication**: personal replies, invitations and content that reach the user at the right moment.

## The feedback loop

A feedback loop connects the layers. Every reply and every invitation becomes new data, and the models retrain on it. Without that loop you have automation. With it you have a growth system.
